Lesson 3.1 Extra Credit — Earth's Resources

Assignment: Sort It: Renewable or Nonrenewable?

A renewable resource is replaced relatively quickly. A nonrenewable resource took millions of years to form and will not be replaced on any useful timescale. Sort each one.

  1. Click Print Blank Copy and write each group name on paper, or choose them on screen.
  2. If you work on screen, use Check My Answers as often as you like.
  3. Write your name and the date on your printed copy.
  4. Submit it using the directions in How to Submit below.
