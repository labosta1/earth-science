What this lesson covers

  • Causes and effects of climate change
  • The greenhouse effect and carbon dioxide
  • Human influence — fossil fuels, deforestation, livestock, and waste
  • Addressing climate change — reforestation, carbon removal, clean energy
  • Science in the news: the future of farming
